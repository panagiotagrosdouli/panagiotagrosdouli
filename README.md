# Panagiota Grosdouli

Electrical and Computer Engineering student at Democritus University of Thrace, focusing on artificial intelligence, robotics, autonomous systems, computer vision, and safety-aware intelligent mobility.

My work explores how AI systems can perceive uncertain environments, reason about risk, and support safer autonomous decision-making in robotics, smart transportation, and human-centered applications.

## Research and Engineering Interests

- Autonomous navigation and robotic planning
- Computer vision and image understanding
- Multi-agent systems and trajectory prediction
- Safety-aware AI for vulnerable road users
- Sensor fusion, SLAM, and uncertainty estimation
- Human-centered AI applications

## Selected Projects

### [DynNav: Dynamic Navigation Rerouting in Unknown Environments](https://github.com/panagiotagrosdouli/DynNav-Dynamic-Navigation-Rerouting-in-Unknown-Environments)
Research-oriented navigation framework for uncertainty-aware, risk-sensitive planning in previously unmapped environments. The project focuses on belief-aware planning, CVaR-style risk reasoning, returnability constraints, and safety monitoring for mobile robots.

### [SafeCrossAI](https://github.com/panagiotagrosdouli/SafeCrossAI)
AI-powered research platform for intelligent urban intersections, focused on vulnerable road user safety, trajectory prediction, risk assessment, and cooperative perception for connected and autonomous mobility.

### [SHIELD-VIO](https://github.com/panagiotagrosdouli/SHIELD-VIO)
Research project related to robust visual-inertial odometry and safety-aware perception for autonomous systems.

### [OpenUWOC-AI](https://github.com/panagiotagrosdouli/OpenUWOC-AI)
Exploratory AI project connected to underwater optical wireless communication and intelligent signal/system modeling.

### [Adaptive Multi-Modal SLAM with Uncertainty-Aware Sensor Fusion](https://github.com/panagiotagrosdouli/Adaptive-Multi-Modal-SLAM-with-Uncertainty-Aware-Sensor-Fusion)
Research prototype investigating adaptive SLAM, uncertainty-aware fusion, and multi-modal sensing for robotic perception.

### [Personal Portfolio](https://github.com/panagiotagrosdouli/personalportfolio)
A Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS academic portfolio website presenting research, projects, CV, and contact information.
