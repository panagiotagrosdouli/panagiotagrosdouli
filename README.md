## Research profile

My research interests lie at the intersection of **robotics, probabilistic perception, planning, sensor fusion, and dependable artificial intelligence**. I am particularly interested in autonomous systems that must operate with incomplete observations, uncertain environment models, changing sensor quality, and safety-critical constraints.

My long-term objective is to contribute to **dependable autonomy**: systems that can quantify uncertainty, detect degradation, explain failure modes, and respond safely when their assumptions no longer hold.

## Featured research project

### [DynNav — Risk-Aware Dynamic Navigation](https://github.com/panagiotagrosdouli/DynNav)

A modular research framework and interactive robotics laboratory for autonomous navigation under uncertainty. DynNav studies risk-aware planning, recoverability, dynamic rerouting, resource constraints, uncertainty estimation, and runtime supervision through reproducible experiments and auditable decision pipelines.

## Research principles

- **Reproducibility:** deterministic configurations, documented assumptions, tests, metrics, and generated artefacts.
- **Uncertainty awareness:** predictions and state estimates should communicate confidence, not only point estimates.
- **Failure-oriented evaluation:** average performance should be complemented by stress tests, rare cases, degradation scenarios, and explicit limitations.
- **Responsible claims:** research prototypes should not be presented as certified safety mechanisms or formal guarantees.
