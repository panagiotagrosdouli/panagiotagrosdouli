## Research Profile

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineer with research interests in **robotics**, **autonomous systems**, **artificial intelligence**, and **machine learning**. My work focuses on developing intelligent robotic systems capable of operating safely and efficiently in uncertain, dynamic, and partially observable environments.

My research direction lies at the intersection of **autonomous navigation**, **motion planning**, **robot perception**, **trajectory prediction**, and **learning-based decision making**. I am particularly interested in how robots can reason about uncertainty, adapt to changing environments, and make safe decisions in real time.

I enjoy building complete research-oriented systems: from problem formulation and algorithmic design to simulation, implementation, experimental evaluation, and scientific documentation.

## Research Direction

My long-term research goal is to contribute to the development of autonomous systems that are:

- **Safe**, especially in uncertain and dynamic environments
- **Adaptive**, through learning and continuous perception
- **Interpretable**, with transparent decision-making mechanisms
- **Robust**, under noisy observations and real-world constraints
- **Human-aware**, when operating around people or in shared spaces

I am especially interested in PhD-level research problems involving **robot learning**, **risk-aware navigation**, **trajectory prediction**, **multi-agent autonomy**, and **AI-driven decision making for cyber-physical systems**.
