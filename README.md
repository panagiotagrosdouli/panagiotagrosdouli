## Research Profile

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineer focused on **Robotics** and **Computer Vision**. My work is centered on how robotic systems perceive their environment, reason about spatial information, and navigate safely in unknown or dynamic settings.

My main research interests lie in **robot perception**, **autonomous navigation**, **motion planning**, **obstacle avoidance**, **image understanding**, and **vision-based intelligent systems**. I am particularly interested in the connection between visual perception and robotic decision making: how machines transform sensory input into safe, reliable, and goal-directed behavior.

I approach projects as research systems: starting from a clearly defined problem, developing the algorithmic pipeline, validating the system experimentally, and documenting results in a way that can support future academic work.

## Featured Projects

### Dynamic Navigation and Rerouting in Unknown Environments

**Repository:** [DynNav-Dynamic-Navigation-Rerouting-in-Unknown-Environments](https://github.com/panagiotagrosdouli/DynNav-Dynamic-Navigation-Rerouting-in-Unknown-Environments)

**Research problem:** How can an autonomous robot navigate safely when the environment is initially unknown and may change during execution?

**Focus areas:**
- Autonomous navigation
- Dynamic path replanning
- Obstacle avoidance
- Robot behavior in uncertain environments

**Scientific relevance:** This project aligns with central problems in mobile robotics, where robots must update their plans as new information becomes available and environmental constraints change.
