# Panagiota Grosdouli

Electrical and Computer Engineering Student
Democritus University of Thrace, Greece

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering student with research interests in **robot perception**, **computer vision**, and **autonomous systems**. My work focuses on enabling autonomous systems to perceive dynamic environments, predict human behaviour, and make reliable decisions under uncertainty.

My current research is centered on trajectory prediction of vulnerable road users for autonomous driving applications. More broadly, I am interested in probabilistic perception, visual-inertial navigation, SLAM, and risk-aware decision making for safety-critical robotic systems.

---

## Current Research

### Diploma Thesis

**Trajectory Prediction of Vulnerable Road Users at Smart Intersections for Road Safety and Autonomous Driving Applications**

My diploma thesis investigates trajectory prediction methods for pedestrians and cyclists operating in complex urban intersections. The objective is to improve prediction reliability in safety-critical environments and support safer decision-making for autonomous driving systems.

The research combines computer vision, trajectory forecasting, and uncertainty-aware perception to study how autonomous systems can better anticipate human motion in dynamic urban environments.

---

## Research Vision

My long-term research goal is to develop autonomous systems that can reliably perceive, predict, and reason about dynamic environments under uncertainty. I am particularly interested in integrating computer vision, probabilistic state estimation, trajectory prediction, and visual-inertial navigation to improve the robustness and safety of autonomous robots.
